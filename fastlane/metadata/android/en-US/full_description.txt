A private, family-run chat server

Family Chat gives each family its own private chat server built on Matrix, the open standard for
real-time communication. Parents manage members and settings from a web control panel; everyone else
just chats.

 • Private group and one-to-one messaging, end-to-end encrypted by default
 • Photos, videos, voice messages and files
 • Emoji reactions, replies, polls and pinned messages
 • Your own domain if you want one

<b>Built for families</b>
No advertising, no advertising identifier, no third-party analytics and no location tracking. Your
messages are never sent to any third-party service for processing.

<b>Own your conversations</b>
Every family has its own server, so your history is yours. Family Chat is open source: this app is a
fork of Element X Android by Element, licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3, and
the source is at https://github.com/unicornops/familychat-android

The application requires the USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT permission so that call notifications arrive even
when the device is locked.
